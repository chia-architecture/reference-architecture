# International before regional before national before local

## Statement

We use internationally agreed solutions first, then regional, then national, then local ones. Each country remains sovereign over its own health system.

## Rationale

Reusing solutions at the highest suitable level gives economies of scale, uniformity and cross-border compatibility. Under subsidiarity, decisions are still taken as close to the country as possible, and regional components are adopted by agreement.

## Implications

- We inventory available international and regional components (e.g. from WHO, PAHO and PH4H) and assess their fit first.
- Regional components are agreed through regional governance and are not imposed.
- National systems connect through agreed interfaces and are not replaced.
- Where possible, we select and procure solutions jointly with other countries.
