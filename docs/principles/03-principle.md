# Incremental and proportionate

## Statement

Interoperability is built step by step, starting from a minimal common base that is sized to the capacity of each country.

## Rationale

Caribbean states differ greatly in size, digital maturity and staffing ([OECD & World Bank 2023](../references.md#ref-oecd2023); [PAHO 2026](../references.md#ref-ops2026)). An architecture that requires everything at once excludes the smallest states. A shared minimal base lets every country join and grow over time.

## Implications

- We define the minimum set of building blocks a country needs to take part.
- Countries can join at different maturity levels and still exchange data.
- Solutions take into account the operational and staffing capacity of small administrations.
- We build on existing national systems and investments instead of replacing them.
