# Standardised

## Statement

We use open, internationally recognised standards to exchange, structure and code health data, at every interoperability layer.

## Rationale

Common standards make exchange between independent systems possible. They reduce dependence on specific vendors and connect the region to the rest of the world.

## Implications

- Designs address all interoperability layers: legal, organisational, semantic and technical ([EC 2017](../references.md#ref-ec2017)).
- We use open international standards such as HL7 FHIR, IHE profiles, SNOMED CT, LOINC and ICD, in line with PH4H ([Ramirez et al. 2026](../references.md#ref-ramirez2026)).
- Processes and data sets are agreed regionally and reused across use cases.
- Data is FAIR: findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable.
- Where no standard exists yet, we weigh the impact of later standardisation before innovating.

<small>Source: Based on ZiRA/Sigra *Gestandaardiseerd* ([Sigra 2025](../references.md#ref-sigra2025)); the Dutch Nictiz five-layer model is replaced by the EIF layers.
