# Continuous and resilient

## Statement

Cross-border health services stay available, including during disasters and disruptions, and changes never put the continuity of care at risk.

## Rationale

The region faces hurricanes, earthquakes, outbreaks and unreliable connectivity. Cross-border data matters most exactly then: during emergencies, evacuations and outbreaks.

## Implications

- Every change is preceded by a risk analysis.
- Critical services are highly available and have fallback procedures, including offline or degraded modes.
- Hosting and backups are distributed, so a single disaster cannot disable the whole region.
- Proven solutions are preferred, and innovations are tested in controlled environments.
- Solutions are documented before production.
- Data is stored so it can be migrated, and an exit strategy is defined before entering a commitment.
