# Architecture principles

This page defines the core principles of the Caribbean Healthcare Interoperability Architecture. These principles guide design decisions, ensure consistency across the architecture, and support cross-border healthcare interoperability. They are intended to provide value for all stakeholders involved.

In these principles, "we" refers to the countries and organisations that take part in cross-border healthcare exchange. The principles are guidelines, not hard rules: they follow the *apply or explain* approach, so a deviation is allowed when it is justified and documented.

Each principle is described by a statement, a rationale and its implications. The set builds on the architecture principles of the Dutch regional reference architecture ([Sigra 2025](../references.md#ref-sigra2025)), which in turn derive from ZiRA. It was compared with other frameworks and adapted to the Caribbean cross-border context: the European Interoperability Framework ([EC 2017](../references.md#ref-ec2017)), the European Health Data Space ([EU 2025](../references.md#ref-ehds2025)), the Dutch national health data network and CumuluZ ([VWS 2025](../references.md#ref-vws2025)), the WHO SMART reference architecture ([WHO n.d.](../references.md#ref-whosmart)) and the Principles for Digital Development ([Digital Impact Alliance n.d.](../references.md#ref-pdd)).
