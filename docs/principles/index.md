# Architecture principles

This page defines the core principles of the Caribbean Healthcare Interoperability Architecture. These principles guide design decisions, ensure consistency across the architecture, and support cross-border healthcare interoperability. They are intended to provide value for all stakeholders involved.

In these principles, "we" refers to the countries and organisations that take part in cross-border healthcare exchange. The principles are guidelines, not hard rules: they follow the *apply or explain* approach, so a deviation is allowed when it is justified and documented.

| # | Principle | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [People-centred](01-principle.md) | People first |
| 2 | [Value-driven](02-principle.md) | Only what adds value |
| 3 | [Incremental and proportionate](03-principle.md) | Step by step, fit for small states |
| 4 | [International before regional before national before local](04-principle.md) | Subsidiarity and sovereignty |
| 5 | [Reuse before buy before build](05-principle.md) | Economies of scale |
| 6 | [Open by default](06-principle.md) | Open standards, software and governance |
| 7 | [Secure and trusted](07-principle.md) | Privacy, security and trust across jurisdictions |
| 8 | [Standardised](08-principle.md) | Open international standards |
| 9 | [Record once at the source, use many times](09-principle.md) | Data quality and ownership |
| 10 | [Modular and decoupled](10-principle.md) | Independent building blocks |
| 11 | [Continuous and resilient](11-principle.md) | Availability in disasters |
